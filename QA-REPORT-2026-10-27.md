## D. Judgement calls, not changed

- In Paper mode the doctor screens are hidden, but the server still accepts orders from a doctor login. This is deliberate: inpatient work and downtime must not stop.
- The login lock-out counts per computer as well as per login: 8 wrong passwords from one desk lock that desk for 10 minutes. This is documented in the handbook. Live trusts the tunnel's forwarded address, so remote users are not locked out together.
- With "Straight away", a lab report can be released before the bill is paid. If you want reports held until payment, that would be a new setting.

| ID | Severity | Finding | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
